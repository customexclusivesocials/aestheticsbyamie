Hi, I’m Amie, the owner of Aesthetics by Amie. I created this space to offer a comfortable, welcoming place where you can slow down, relax, and enjoy the “me time” you deserve.

I believe self-care doesn’t have to be expensive or over-the-top to make a difference. My goal is to provide personalized, quality treatments that help you feel confident, refreshed, and cared for — every step of the way.

Alongside my treatments, I’m also an Aloette skincare consultant and love helping clients find products that feel right for their skin and routine.
